## Findings
- Highest-revenue city: Delhi
- Highest-profit category: Furniture
- Highest-profit product: Desk
- Highest-revenue month: April month
- Month with largest revenue decline: May
- Highest-spending customer: Amit spent ₹15400.00

## Interpretation and Caveats

### Key Interpretations
- **High-Margin Category Driver:** Furniture is the primary driver of profitability, led specifically by Desks, yielding an overall healthy profit margin of 35.79%.

- **Geographic and Customer Concentration:** Revenue shows notable concentration in Delhi, with a significant share contributed by a single top customer (Amit, accounting for ₹15,400 of the ₹48,900 total revenue, or ~31.5%).

- **Revenue Volatility:** Performance fluctuates sharply month-over-month, peaking in April before experiencing its steepest decline in May.

### Caveats & Data Limitations
- **Small Sample Size:** The analysis is based on only 20 records and 110 units sold, meaning individual transactions heavily skew the aggregate KPIs.

- **Synthetic Dataset:** This data is entirely simulated for demonstration purposes and cannot be used to infer real-world retail trends, market demand, or consumer purchasing behavior.
